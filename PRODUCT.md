# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gamers, testers, and people automating repetitive Android interactions while another app remains visible. They configure actions directly against the live screen they intend to automate.

## Product Purpose

Klick'r makes precise Android automation approachable, combining screen-aware triggers with configurable gestures. Success means users can build and verify an automation without losing the visual context of the target app.

## Brand Personality

Practical, precise, unobtrusive. Controls should feel dependable and disappear behind the automation task.

## Anti-references

Opaque configuration screens that hide the target app, decorative control surfaces, and unfamiliar interactions that diverge from Klick'r's existing floating overlay menus.

## Design Principles

- Keep the target app visible whenever screen position matters.
- Reuse familiar overlay controls and interaction patterns.
- Show selection state directly where automation will run.
- Prevent invalid configuration before confirmation.
- Preserve user work when an editing flow is cancelled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Android semantic labels, large touch targets, high-contrast selection states, and more than color alone to communicate validity. Keep interactions usable across supported screen sizes and orientations.
